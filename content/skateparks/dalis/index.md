## Location

2 Bristol Ln, Ellicottville, NY 14731

## Description

Indoor skatepark in Ellicottville.
In the space formerly occupied by Daily City Train, which closed in fall 2025.
Dali's opened October 2026.

### Website

[dalisindoorpark.com](https://www.dalisindoorpark.com/)

## Features

- 4' and 7' quarter pipes
- funbox with down rail and ledge
- pyramid with ledge
- box
