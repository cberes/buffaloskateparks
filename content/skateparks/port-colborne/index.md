## Location

133 Mellanby Ave, Port Colborne, ON L3K 3T9, Canada

## Description

Skatepark with a unique nautical theme next to the canal.
Half of the park has street features and the other half has big transitions.

## Features

- 2' and 4' quarter pipes
- 6' to 8' bowl corners and quarter pipes
- hip
- box
- manual pad
- kicker
- flat rail
- down rail
- 7-stair with hand rail and hubba
- euro gap
- pole jam
- pump track
