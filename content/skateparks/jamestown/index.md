## Location 

117 Fairmount Ave, Jamestown, NY 14701

## Description

Opened in [October 2022](https://www.wrfalp.com/grand-opening-held-for-new-skatepark/). Huge, unique skatepark with two bowls, two street sections, and big transitions.

### Parking

Parking is available at McCrea Point Park (down Fairmount Ave on the other side of the Chadakoin River).

### Bathrooms

There are bathrooms by the parking lot in McCrea Point Park.

## Features

### Top level

- box
- manual pad
- flat rail
- bank
- 3' quarter pipe with pool coping

### Street bowl

- 3' to 5' china banks
- 6' quarter pipes
- 7' quarter pipe (east wall)
- A-frame with rail and bump
- 4-stair
- down rail
- euro gap
- hip
- bank to jersey barrier

### Middle bowl

- 6' bowl
- 7' quarter pipe (east wall)
- 8' bank wall (west wall)
- roller
- wrench vert extension

### Far bowl

- 4' to 5' bowl with pool coping
- 8' corner with vert
- waterfall
- 5' island with pool coping and roll-in on the north wall
