## Location

Ridgecrest Park, 985 Ebner Dr, Webster, NY 14580

## Description

Modular skatepark, though the surface is decently smooth.
There’s a large area for flat-ground skating.

## Features

- 3' quarter pipe and 3' spine (on opposite sides of the park)
- 5' miniramp
- large bank
- funbox with rails, ledges, and euro gap
- another funbox with a ledge
- round and square flat rails
- weird double round rail
- box
