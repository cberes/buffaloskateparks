## Location

7150 Montrose Rd, Niagara Falls, ON L2H 3N3, Canada

## Description

Aging skatepark with lots of banks, rails, and boxes.
One corner has a 3-block and stair sets but not much in terms of run-up.
There's about 80% of a bowl.
