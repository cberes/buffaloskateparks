## Location

Jefferson Memorial Park, 329 Jefferson Ave, Canandaigua, NY 14424

## Description

There's a limited variety of obstacles, but there are some unique obstacles.
There's no proper manual pad, box, or flat rail.
Also the stairs have little run-up and may be unusable.
However, the manual pad has a slight grade, which is neat.
The walls of the miniramp meet with banks to form hips.
And there's a 4' quarter-to-quarter (like an elongated spine).

## Features

- 4' miniramp
- 4' quarter pipes
- A-frame with rail and ledges
- 6-stair with hand rail
- angled manual pad
- manual pad to tall ledge
- hips
- roller

## Lighting

There are overhead lights around the park.
