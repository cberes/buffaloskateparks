## Location

Perinton Park, 99 O'Connor Rd, Fairport, NY 14450

## Description

There's a huge pump track, which is neat, but scooter kids love it.
It starts on the west end of the park and lets out on the east end of the park.
Unfortunately kids then ride through the whole park to get back to the pump track.
Also there were some questionable choices, such as quarter pipes that are too small,
and a larger quarter pipe that's opposite the turf section.
