## Location

Welch Field, Union St & Elm St, Westfield, NY

## Description

Pre-fab concrete features on a rough asphalt surface.
The surface is worse than Evans Skatepark but better than Orchard Park.

## Features

- 3' quarter pipes
- large A-frame
- box
- manual pad
- round rail (curved)

## Lighting

There are overhead lights around the park.
