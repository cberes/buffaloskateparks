## Location

240 St Paul St W, St. Catharines, ON L2S 2E7, Canada

## Description

Big skatepark with some small obstacles, some big obstacles, and very little in between. The concrete is starting to get a little rough.

### Bathrooms

There are bathrooms in the adjacent ice rink.

## Features

- 3' rounded quarter pipe
- 6' quarter pipe
- pyramids (one with a flat rail, one with A-frame ledge)
- hips
- boxes
- 9-stair with hand rail, hubbas, and step-up
- 5-stair with hand rail and hubbas
- 3-stair with hand rail and hubbas
- 2-stair with manual pad
- flat rails
- down rail
