## Location

241 South Ave, Rochester, NY 14604

## Description

Concrete skatepark in downtown Rochester.
Part of it is under the I-490.

The [phase 2](https://www.cityofrochester.gov/departments/department-environmental-services-des/roc-city-skatepark-phase-2-development) update is currently underway.

### Parking

There's no dedicated parking lot.

## Features

- bowl
- 2' miniramp
- 4' and 5' quarter pipes
- A-frame with rail and combination ledge and quarter pipe
- curved box
- box
- manual pad
- flat rail
- slightly down rail
- slightly down ledges
- 8-stair with hand rail and hubbas
- 6-stair with hand rail and hubba

## Lighting

There are overhead lights around the park.
