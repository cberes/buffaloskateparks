## Location

Greece Town Hall, 3 Vince Tofany Blvd, Rochester, NY 14612

## Description

Large concrete skatepark with an ancient-Greece theme in the Rochester suburbs.
It's behind the town hall (past the tennis courts).
Greece has a great library&mdash;be sure to stop by.

### Bathrooms

There are bathrooms in the senior center and the library.
