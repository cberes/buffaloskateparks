## Location

Bayview Rd, Hamburg, NY 14219

## Description

**As of spring 2026 the skatepark has been dismantled.** Hamburg's new [concrete skatepark](/skateparks/hamburg) has opened.

Modular ramps on a rough asphalt surface. The ramps are in bad shape.

## Features

- 4' miniramp
- 3' miniramp
- 3' spine
- funbox with rail
- 3' quarter pipe
